Schedule reviews of investment assumptions

Inputs become stale at different rates. A signed financing term, an operating quote and an annual revenue estimate should each have a review trigger appropriate to the decision they support. Use a refresh calendar to identify when the model must be updated before the next approval.

Work through the decision

  1. Assign a review trigger to material inputs.
  2. Name the person who will obtain updates.
  3. Record changes and unchanged confirmations.
  4. Refresh the decision model before reuse.

For each action, name the person responsible for preparing the response and the person who approves it. Record what evidence would close the item. If new information changes cost, timing or scope, update the affected plan and identify who needs the revised version.

Common failure to avoid

A recently edited spreadsheet can still contain old inputs. Track the source date rather than relying on the file modification date.
