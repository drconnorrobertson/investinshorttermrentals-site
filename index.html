The process

How a done-for-you STR investment actually gets built

  • Understand your goal. Cash flow, tax offset, long-term equity, or some blend -- the target shapes everything after.
  • Source and underwrite. Roughly 1,000 listings screened weekly; about 2% clear full underwriting.
  • Structure. Financing, entity structure and tax modeling are built around your specific numbers.
  • Acquire and set up. Closing, design and furnishing are coordinated to get the property guest-ready quickly.
  • Operate and scale. Self-manage, co-host or fully outsource -- and revisit the plan as you consider a second property.

Who this is for

High-income W-2 earners and business owners who want real estate exposure and the tax benefits that come with active short-term rental participation, without spending a year learning underwriting and revenue management first.

If you're evaluating your first investment or your fifth, the underwriting standard is the same.

Frequently asked

How much capital do I need to get started?

It depends on market and financing structure, and is discussed on your consultation call once your goals and budget are understood.

Is this passive?

Short-term rental investing generally requires more involvement than a passive fund, though management can be outsourced. Some tax benefits specifically depend on material participation, which is discussed on the call.

Do you find the property or do I?

Both paths work -- the acquisitions team can source from its own pipeline, or underwrite a property you've already found, to the same standard.

What markets do you invest in?

The team currently sources across eight states selected for demand, regulation and supply fundamentals; your target market is confirmed on the call.
