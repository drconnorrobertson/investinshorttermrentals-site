Investment memo

Five questions before capital moves.

  1. Why this asset class?
    Clarify what direct ownership is expected to contribute to the portfolio.
  2. Why this market?
    Test demand durability, regulation, supply growth and seasonality.
  3. What breaks the case?
    Model lower revenue, higher expenses, slower launch and refinancing friction.
  4. Who operates it?
    Assign responsibility, cost and accountability for every recurring function.
  5. How does it end?
    Understand resale liquidity, alternate uses and the intended hold period.
