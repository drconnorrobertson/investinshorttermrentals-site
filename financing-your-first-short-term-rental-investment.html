Financing Your First Short-Term Rental Investment

Published 2026-01-01 · BNB Accelerator Editorial Team

DSCR loans qualify the deal on the property's income

Debt-service coverage ratio loans underwrite primarily against the property's projected income relative to its debt payment, which makes them a common path for investors acquiring a short-term rental as an investment rather than a primary residence.

Rates and down payment requirements are typically less favorable than an owner-occupant conventional loan, and lenders often apply a conservative income estimate specifically for short-term rental properties given their revenue variability.

Conventional and portfolio financing remain viable paths

A conventional investment property loan, underwritten against the borrower's personal income and credit, can offer better terms than a DSCR loan for investors who qualify, though it typically comes with stricter debt-to-income requirements.

Comparing financing paths against your specific numbers -- not defaulting to whichever loan type is most commonly discussed -- is part of structuring the investment correctly from the start.
