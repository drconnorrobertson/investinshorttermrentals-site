How to Vet a Short-Term Rental Investment Market

Published 2026-09-25 · Invest in Short Term Rentals Editorial

Demand durability comes first

A market worth investing in needs a demand driver that isn't a single fragile trend -- tourism, outdoor recreation, event calendars, business travel, or a combination, ideally with more than one source of visitor demand supporting occupancy across the year.

A market that boomed on a single viral moment or a temporary trend is a much riskier long-term bet than one with multiple durable demand sources.

Regulation and supply determine whether the demand is investable

Even strong demand doesn't matter if local regulation makes new short-term rental permits scarce, restricted to owner-occupants, or subject to a pending moratorium. And a market can be flooded with existing short-term rental supply to the point that average daily rates are compressed regardless of demand.

Checking all three -- demand, regulation, supply -- together, rather than any one in isolation, is the actual definition of vetting a market.
