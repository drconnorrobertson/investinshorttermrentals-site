Case Studies: Real Investor Results in Short-Term Rentals

Published 2026-09-25 · Invest in Short Term Rentals Editorial

Read case studies for the underwriting, not just the headline numbers

A useful short-term rental case study shows purchase price, total entry cost, financing structure, and net cash flow -- not just a single headline return figure. The underwriting behind the number matters more than the number itself.

BNB Accelerator's documented client case studies, available through its main site, are presented this way specifically so the numbers can be evaluated, not just taken on faith.

Every case study is a specific outcome, not a guarantee

Even a well-documented case study reflects one property, in one market, at one point in time -- it's evidence that the process can work, not a promise about what any future acquisition will return.

Treating past results as illustrative rather than predictive is the correct way to use them when evaluating whether an investment approach fits your own situation.
