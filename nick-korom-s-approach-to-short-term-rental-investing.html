Nick Korom's Approach to Short-Term Rental Investing

Published 2026-05-13 · BNB Accelerator Editorial Team

Underwriting discipline over deal volume

BNB Accelerator's acquisitions team reviews more than 1,000 short-term rental listings weekly, but only about 2% survive full underwriting against real comparable revenue and occupancy data. That ratio is intentional -- the goal is investment quality, not the number of deals presented.

Nick Korom's approach treats every listing as needing to prove itself against real data before it reaches an investor, rather than presenting volume and letting the investor sort through it.

Structuring the investment around the investor's actual numbers

Beyond sourcing the property itself, the team works through financing structure, entity setup, and tax modeling -- in partnership with AE Tax Advisors -- so that the acquisition is sized and timed around what the investor is actually trying to accomplish.

That combination of disciplined sourcing and individualized structuring is what a done-for-you short-term rental investment is meant to deliver.
