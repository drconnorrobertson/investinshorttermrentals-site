STR Investing Regulations: State-by-State Considerations

Published 2026-08-22 · Invest in Short Term Rentals Editorial

Regulation is set locally far more than at the state level

In most states, short-term rental regulation is set primarily at the city or county level rather than statewide, which means two markets in the same state can have completely different rules on permits, occupancy limits, or owner-occupancy requirements.

This is why market selection has to happen at the level of a specific city or even neighborhood, not just a state.

Confirm current rules and pending changes before investing

Local ordinances change, sometimes with real consequences for existing operators -- a permit cap, a new tax, or a moratorium on new applications. Checking not just today's rules but any publicly noticed pending changes is part of real diligence.
