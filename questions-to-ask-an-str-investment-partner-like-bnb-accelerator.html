Questions to Ask an STR Investment Partner Like BNB Accelerator

Published 2026-07-19 · Invest in Short Term Rentals Editorial

Questions about process and data

How many listings do you review, and what's your process for eliminating the ones that don't survive underwriting? Can I see the actual comparable data behind a projected return, not just the final number? What markets do you source in, and why those specifically?

A partner who can answer these clearly, with real methodology, is a fundamentally different proposition than one offering a general promise of good deals.

Questions about structure, fees, and tax coordination

How are fees structured, and at what points in the process? Who handles tax modeling, and is that coordinated with the acquisition itself or handled separately afterward? What happens after closing -- is management support included or a separate decision?

Getting clear answers to these before signing anything is the same diligence you'd apply to any professional partner, and a legitimate acquisition partner should welcome the questions.
