STR Investing Risks and How to Manage Them

Published 2026-04-10 · Invest in Short Term Rentals Editorial

Revenue volatility and regulatory risk are the two big ones

Short-term rental revenue is inherently more seasonal and demand-sensitive than a long-term lease, and local regulation can change the rules on permits, occupancy limits, or taxation with real consequences for an existing investment.

Both risks are manageable, not avoidable -- revenue volatility through conservative underwriting and cash reserves, regulatory risk through market selection and ongoing monitoring of local ordinances.

Concentration and management risk round out the list

A portfolio concentrated in a single market carries more risk than one diversified across markets with different demand drivers. And management risk -- whether self-managed or outsourced -- is real: a poorly managed property underperforms even in a strong market.

A structured acquisition process addresses these risks at the underwriting stage, rather than leaving an investor to discover them after closing.
